Clyvora Technologies builds private browser tools.

Clyvora—also known as Clyvora Tech or Clyvora Technologies—creates free, open-source tools that process data and media on your device instead of uploading selected files to a processing server.

Use a Clyvora tool

  • Clyvora Lens — inspect, search, filter, and convert JSON and CSV files locally in your browser.
  • Clyvora Convert — convert images, audio, and video locally in your browser.
